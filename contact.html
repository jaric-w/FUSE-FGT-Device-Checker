Getting in

Please read this before your first visit.

  • Use the shop entrance

    It's on the northeast corner of the building. Please don't use the residential entrance.

  • There are stairs

    The entrance isn't wheelchair accessible. Call ahead and we'll help however we can.

  • Bringing a lot?

    We have carts and people to help unload. For a big batch from a business, book a pickup instead.

Common questions

Answers from Free Geek Toronto's FAQ.

When can I drop off tech?

Fridays and Saturdays, 12:15 to 4:45 PM. Use the donation checker first to make sure we can take it.

Do you ship computers?

We ship laptops within Canada by Canada Post. Everything else is bought and picked up at the shop.

Can my organization buy computers in bulk?

Yes. Charities, nonprofits, grassroots and social change groups can buy low-cost computers in bulk through our Technology for Social Change program.

How do I start volunteering?

Register for an online volunteer info session. They're held most months, usually on a Wednesday evening. More about volunteering.

Will my data be safe?

Yes. Every item we reuse goes through industry-standard data destruction first.
