Your data is erased first

Before any computer is reused, its drive is wiped with industry-standard data destruction. Drives that can't be wiped are destroyed.

You don't need to do anything first, but it's still a good idea to back up your files and sign out of your accounts.

Donation questions

Still unsure? Call 416-572-3737 during shop hours.

Do you take broken computers?

Yes. Computers that can't be refurbished give us parts, and the rest is sent for responsible recycling.

Does it cost anything to donate?

Drop-off at the shop is always free. Pickup for large batches starts at $60 depending on the area, and businesses giving 10 or more similar, working computers get it free.

Should I bring the chargers?

Please do. A laptop with its own charger is much quicker to refurbish and send out as a complete setup.

What if my item isn't on the list?

Call us before you bring it. We'll tell you whether we can take it, or where it can go instead.
