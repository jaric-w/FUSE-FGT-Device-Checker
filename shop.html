What's usually in stock

Our shop runs entirely on donations, so what's on the shelves changes every week.

  • Laptops and desktops

    Wiped, repaired and ready to use, most running Linux Mint. Laptops can be shipped anywhere in Canada.

  • Monitors, keyboards and mice

    Everything you need to turn a desktop into a full setup.

  • Computer parts

    Some memory, drives and cards for repairs and upgrades. Ask what we have.

  • Phones and tablets

    Mostly phones, tested and reset.

  • Gaming consoles

    Consoles and games when they come in.

  • TV and home theatre

    Flat-screen TVs, speakers, receivers and players.

Why Linux Mint?

Most of our computers come with Linux Mint, a free operating system that's easy to use.

  • It runs well on older computersWhich is most of what we receive.
  • No account neededYour computer works without signing in to a cloud service.
  • Your usual browser worksFirefox, Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera all run on it.
  • It's a career skillLinux runs most of the internet's servers.
