Computers for everyone.

Free Geek Toronto is an independent not-for-profit in Parkdale. We turn donated tech into affordable computers, job skills and less e-waste.

Why we exist

Today you need a computer to apply for jobs, do homework, see a doctor or reach family. Many people in Toronto still can't afford one.

At the same time, companies and households throw away working computers every day. We sit in the middle: we take what people no longer need, make it work again, and sell it at a price that reflects what people can pay.

Our vision is a Toronto where everyone has an affordable way to get online, and where old tech is reused instead of dumped.

What we do

Four programs, one goal: more people online and less tech in landfill.

  • Tech reuse and recycling

    We accept unwanted electronics and use industry-standard data destruction on everything we reuse. What can't be reused is recycled responsibly.

  • Refurbished computer sales

    Used computers, electronics and parts at accessible prices, in our Parkdale shop and online.

  • Training and mentoring

    Volunteers and paid trainees learn repair, sales, customer service and warehousing, skills for today's digital jobs.

  • Technology for social change

    Low-cost computers in bulk for charities, nonprofits and community groups.

Our story in numbers

Free Geek Toronto runs as an employment social enterprise: the shop is also a training ground.

  1. Since 2016

    66 people have trained with us, most of them earning wages while they learn. Together they've been paid over $450,000.

  2. 2019 to 2023

    Over 1,200 computers refurbished. About 55% went to low-income households, people on social assistance and our Hardware Grant program for under $200.

  3. Today

    More than 2,000 computers refurbished or rebuilt, and over 400,000 pounds of electronics sent for ethical recycling.
